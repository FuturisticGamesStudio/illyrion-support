Illyrion Support

Illyrion is a low-poly, top-down space survival arcade game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Futuristic Games Studio.

Contact

For help, questions, bug reports or feedback, email us at futuristicgames98@gmail.com. We'll do our best to reply as soon as possible.

When reporting a bug, it helps to include your device model, and what happened right before the problem.

Common questions

  • I reinstalled the Game and lost my ships. Purchased items come back with "Restore Purchases" in the Shop. Ships unlocked by score or ads are saved only on your device.
  • No ad is available. Ads aren't always available. Please try again a little later and check your internet connection.
  • How do I delete my Rankings data? Use "Delete account" in Rankings, or email us with your nickname if you no longer have the Game.
  • How do I change my personalized ads choice? Open Settings > Privacy in the Game.
