Illyrion Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Illyrion (the "Game") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is made by Futuristic Games Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at futuristicgames98@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, unlocked ships, settings) is saved only on your device. We also save on your device:

  • whether you are under 13 (we do not store your birth year or age),
  • that you accepted this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use,
  • your personalized ads choice.

Deleting the Game removes this information from your device.

2. Online Rankings

If you join Rankings, we use Unity Gaming Services (by Unity Technologies) to create an anonymous player ID and to store your nickname, your score and the ship you used. Your nickname, score and ship are shown publicly to other players in Rankings.

  • Players 13 and older type their own nickname. Please do not use your real name or any personal information in your nickname.
  • Players under 13 cannot type a nickname. They pick one from ready-made word lists (for example "SwiftFalcon72"), so no personal information is shared.
  • Nicknames that break our rules may be blocked, and repeat offenders may be removed from Rankings. See the Terms of Use.

3. Analytics

We use Unity Analytics (by Unity Technologies) to understand how the Game is played, for example how long sessions last, which features are used, and where players stop playing. This helps us fix problems and improve the Game. Analytics uses an anonymous identifier and basic device information (such as device model, operating system and country). We do not use analytics to identify you personally.

4. Ads

The Game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch an ad to get a reward). Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, which works with these ad partners:

  • ironSource Ads and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies)
  • Mintegral
  • Liftoff
  • DT Exchange (Digital Turbine)
  • InMobi

To show and measure ads, these partners may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, language and general location (country or region), and how you interact with ads.

  • Your choice: the first time you open the Game, players 13 and older choose "Allow" or "No thanks" for personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy. You see ads either way. "No thanks" means ads are not based on how you use apps.
  • iPhone and iPad: if you choose "Allow", Apple then asks whether the Game may track you. You can change this later in your device's Settings.
  • Players under 13: ads are never personalized, Apple's tracking request is never shown, and we tell our ad partners that the player is a child so they treat the request as child-directed.

Each ad partner handles information under its own privacy policy, which you can find on its website.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which items you bought, so we can unlock them and restore them with "Restore Purchases".

6. Children

The Game is for everyone, including children. We ask for the player's birth year in a neutral way when the Game is first opened. For players under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information: they cannot type a nickname, ads are not personalized, and no tracking request is shown. Anonymous identifiers are used only to run the Game, Rankings and analytics. If you are a parent and believe your child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the Game: use Delete account in Rankings to remove your nickname, score and player ID.
  • By email: if you no longer have the Game, email futuristicgames98@gmail.com with your Rankings nickname, and we will delete your Rankings data.

Information on your device is removed when you delete the Game. To limit ad tracking, you can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device's settings.

8. How long we keep information

Rankings data is kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account. Analytics data is kept only as long as needed to improve the Game, under Unity's retention rules.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can change your ads choice in Settings > Privacy, delete your Rankings account in the Game, or email us. We do not sell your personal information for money.

10. Security

We use trusted services (Unity, Apple and Google) that protect information with industry-standard security. No system is 100% secure, but we only keep what the Game needs.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For important changes, the Game will ask you to accept the new version.
